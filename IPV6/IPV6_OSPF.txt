In this Scenario We are going to use IPV6 OSPF

============R1 Configs==============
en 
conf t
hostname R1
ipv6 unicast-routing 
int g 0/0/0
no sh
ipv6 address 2001:a:b:12::2/64
exit
ipv6 router ospf 1 
router-id 1.1.1.1
exit
int g 0/0/0
ipv6 ospf 1 area 1
do wr

============R2 Configs==============
