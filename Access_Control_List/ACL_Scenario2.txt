*for having a wild card that include two IP's (Condition 2 on first part) we use this :

10.1.1.100
00001010.00000001.00000001.01100100   0

10.1.1.101
00001010.00000001.00000001.01100101   1

The two addresses differ only in the last bit:

10.1.1.100 → ...01100100
10.1.1.101 → ...01100101
                         ↑
                    only this bit

Therefore:

Network: 10.1.1.100/31
Mask:    255.255.255.254
Wildcard: 0.0.0.1
